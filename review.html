Anon_lmaolmao

I’ve been using Ecran sunscreen for a while now because I like its cream-like texture; it leaves no white cast, doesn’t pill, and has a matte finish. It feels quite similar to this Manic sunscreen, though Manic has a thinner consistency and is easier to blend. Both are hybrid sunscreens. However, I like that Manic uses two physical sunscreen filters (zinc oxide and titanium dioxide), whereas Ecran only uses zinc oxide. These ingredients are expensive, which is likely why Manic costs more. I bought Manic for 532 (on sale) and Ecran for around 480+. I still need to test this Manic sunscreen throughout the day to see if it makes my skin oily or causes breakouts. Ecran claims to be non-comedogenic, though they haven’t released any test results to support that. So far, it’s been fine; based on the ingredients, I’d lean toward Manic to ensure better protection. If I end up breaking out, I might just go back to Ecran. We’ll see!
